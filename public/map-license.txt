World map

The map of the real world in 대륙 쟁탈전 (Continent Conquest) is drawn from
Natural Earth's 1:50m admin-0 countries, as published at
https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector (master branch, version
5.2.0-pre, file geojson/ne_50m_admin_0_countries.geojson, downloaded
2026-11-19).

Made with Natural Earth. Free vector and raster map data @ naturalearthdata.com.
Natural Earth data is in the public domain: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

Changes: the countries are grouped into 48 regions of our own on six
continents, the largest countries cut along straight lines of our own, the
outlines projected (Robinson, the north let out a little and the crowded seas
magnified), simplified and rounded by scripts/build-world.mjs, which writes
src/games/conquest/world-data.ts. The mountain ranges painted as relief are
traced by hand from general knowledge of geography.
