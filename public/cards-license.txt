Playing card images in Blackjack and Seven-Card Stud

The fifty-two card faces are reduced-size renderings of the SVG files
"English pattern <rank> of <suit>.svg" from Wikimedia Commons, by Dmitry Fomin
(Дмитрий Фомин), released under the Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal Public
Domain Dedication, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:English_pattern_playing_cards
Changes: rendered at 250 px width on a white ground and re-encoded as WebP;
in the games the faces are drawn with a paper tone multiplied over them.
