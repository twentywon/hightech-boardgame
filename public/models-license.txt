3D models in 시대 전쟁 (era war) — the people
=============================================

The soldiers of 시대 전쟁 are built from these artist-made models, all by Quaternius (https://quaternius.com),
dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
Blender poses, trims, recolours and thins them, and adds the helms, hats, vests, belts and side bands
(tools/blender/erawar/assets in the source).

- Universal Base Characters (Standard, free) — the male base body, head, eyes, eyebrows, hairstyles
  (Hair_Long, Hair_Buzzed, Hair_SimpleParted, Hair_Beard) and their pictures.
  https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-base-characters — CC0 1.0
- Modular Character Outfits - Fantasy (Standard, free) — the male peasant and ranger outfits and their pictures
  (the peasant's shirt and sleeves and the ranger's greens dyed in each side's colour, the peasant also in olive drab).
  https://quaternius.itch.io/modular-character-outfits-fantasy — CC0 1.0
