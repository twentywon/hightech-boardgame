Recorded sound effects

The dice, card and chip sounds are taken from "Casino Audio" (1.1) by Kenney
Vleugels (www.kenney.nl), released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0),
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Source: https://opengameart.org/content/54-casino-sound-effects-cards-dice-chips
Changes: twelve of the files were converted from Ogg Vorbis to 22.05 kHz mono
WAV, trimmed and levelled (src/engine/sounds/).

The sounds of wood, stone, tiles, glass, cloth, paper and coins are taken
from two more packs by Kenney Vleugels (www.kenney.nl), both released under
Creative Commons Zero (CC0):

- "RPG Audio" (50 RPG sound effects),
  https://opengameart.org/content/50-rpg-sound-effects
  (knock-*, soft-*, paper-*, coin-*, glass-4)
- "UI Audio" (51 UI sound effects: buttons, switches and clicks),
  https://opengameart.org/content/51-ui-sound-effects-buttons-switches-and-clicks
  (stone-*, tile-*, glass-1 to glass-3)

Changes: thirty-three of the files were converted to 22.05 kHz mono WAV,
trimmed, levelled and renamed after what they stand for in the games
(src/engine/sounds/).

The sounds below were recorded by Joseph Sardin for BigSoundBank
(https://bigsoundbank.com), released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0):

- wood-1 to wood-4: "Two dice on a wooden table" (s0581), single knocks
- stick-1 to stick-4: "Drumsticks 1" (s1488) and "Drumsticks 3" (s1969)
- marble-1: "Glass ball bounce 1" (s2240)
- sizzle-1 to sizzle-3: "Frying pan 2" (s0144) and "Frying pan 3" (s0145)
- chew-1 to chew-3: "Mouth noises 1" to "3" (s0352, s0353, s0354)
- bark-2: "Barking dog" (s0916)
- hop-1: "Steps in the grass, quick" (s1254)
- pour-1: "Cold water in mug" (s1156)
- gurgle-1: "Gurgling sink 1" (s1026)

The billiard sounds (ball-*, cushion-*, pocket-1) are taken from "Pool And
Billiards SFX" by Suraj Kispotta (papapleygames), released under Creative
Commons Zero (CC0 1.0): https://papapleygames.itch.io/pool-and-billiards

Changes to all of these: cut to single strokes or short passages, faded,
levelled and converted to 22.05 kHz mono WAV or MP3 (src/engine/sounds/).

The background music is not a recording: it is made by the page as it plays
(src/engine/music.ts).

Two more recordings by Joseph Sardin for BigSoundBank (https://bigsoundbank.com),
released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0), for battleship (the user asked
for a real splash and a real explosion):

- splash-1: "Splash big 3" (s1521), trimmed to its first splash and faded
- explosion-1: "Explosion 1" (s1807) laid over "Explosion far away" (s1023),
  its low end raised, trimmed and faded

Both were mixed to 22.05 kHz mono MP3 and levelled (src/engine/sounds/).

The arcade tables (bowling, air hockey, basketball, the claw machine and
whack-a-mole) were given sounds of their own. Every one is made from
recordings released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0); none of them is
a recording of a bowling alley, an air hockey table or a basketball machine
(no free recording of those was found), so each is put together from the
nearest real sounds:

- Recorded by Joseph Sardin for BigSoundBank (https://bigsoundbank.com):
  "Juggling 3 clubs" (s1805), "Plastic cup falling" (s0630), "Ball Kicked"
  (s1044), "Flag #2" (s1788), "Electric car window, opening #1" (s1502) and
  "Little electric winch" (s0708)
- Recorded by Clarisse for BigSoundBank: "Poker Chips" (s0942)
- From "Impact Sounds" (1.0) by Kenney Vleugels (www.kenney.nl),
  https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds: impactWood_heavy, impactWood_medium,
  impactGeneric_light, impactMetal_medium, impactPlank_medium_001 and
  impactPunch_heavy_000
- "Bowling Ball Rolling" by qubodup (Iwan Gabovitch), OpenGameArt,
  https://opengameart.org/content/bowling-ball-rolling

What each file is:

- pins-1 to pins-3 (bowling): single knocks of the juggling clubs (hollow,
  pin-shaped), played lower to a pin's size and laid one after another as a
  rack falls (24 for a strike, 9 for a few pins, 2 for a nudge), over a heavy
  wooden thump and a few wooden knocks on the deck, with a hall's echo added
- puck-1 to puck-3 (air hockey): the poker chips' clack played lower over a
  light knock (the mallet), a duller and shorter knock (a rail), and the
  plastic cup falling, played lower over a thump (a goal's chute)
- bounce-1, hoop-1, board-1 (basketball): the kicked ball's thump and the ring
  of its air, played lower to a basketball's; with a metal clang over it for
  the rim, and with a plank's knock for the backboard
- swish-1 (basketball): the nylon flag shaken once, its low end taken off
- roll-1 (basketball): the ball rolling, played higher, as a seamless loop
- motor-1, winch-1 (claw machine): steady stretches of the window motor and
  the winch, as seamless loops
- clasp-1 (claw machine): a metal knock over a wooden thump
- thud-1 (whack-a-mole): impactPunch_heavy_000 as it is

Changes to all of these: cut, pitched, mixed, faded, levelled and converted
to 22.05 kHz mono MP3 (src/engine/sounds/).

Recorded music (src/engine/tracks/)

- home.mp3 (the collection's music) and era-war.mp3 (시대 전쟁's music) are
  recordings the app's owner supplied for these places; they are not CC0 and
  not part of the packs above. Changes: re-encoded to 44.1 kHz stereo MP3
