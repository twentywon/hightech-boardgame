Photographed textures

The table top of the meat grill is the diffuse map of "Wood Table" by
Dimitrios Savva (Poly Haven, https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_table), released
under Creative Commons Zero (CC0),
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Changes: reduced to 768 x 768 and stored as WebP (src/games/grill/table.webp).

The raw flesh of four of its cuts is cut out of three photographs from the
WordPress Photo Directory (https://wordpress.org/photos/), all released
under Creative Commons Zero (CC0):

- pork belly: photograph by Yam B Chhetri,
  https://wordpress.org/photos/photo/5306a119c9/
- marinated rib: photograph by Yam B Chhetri,
  https://wordpress.org/photos/photo/3076a11a25/
- brisket and neck: photograph by Vishnu Chandra,
  https://wordpress.org/photos/photo/9496a09869/

Changes: a square of flesh taken from each, reduced to 320 x 320 and stored
as WebP (src/games/grill/belly.webp, rib.webp, brisket.webp, neck.webp); the
neck made paler; the colour of cooking is laid over them by the game.

The lettuce and the four relishes are cut out of two more photographs from
the WordPress Photo Directory, both CC0:

- kimchi, bean paste, garlic with green chilli, salt in oil: photograph by
  Yam B Chhetri, https://wordpress.org/photos/photo/3226a9519c/
- lettuce (the basket and the leaf of a wrap): photograph by Bigul Malayi,
  https://wordpress.org/photos/photo/326901e913/

Changes: a square taken from each, reduced and stored as WebP
(src/games/grill/kimchi.webp, paste.webp, garlic.webp, oil.webp,
lettuce.webp, leaf.webp); the oil and the lettuce made lighter.

Wood, veneer and cloth of the other tables are Poly Haven textures
(https://polyhaven.com), all released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0),
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

- "Dark Wood" by Dario Barresi, Dimitrios Savva and Rico Cilliers
  (https://polyhaven.com/a/dark_wood): colour, normal and roughness maps
  (src/assets/tex/wood-dark.webp, wood-normal.webp, wood-rough.webp)
- "Oak Veneer 01" by Jenelle van Heerden
  (https://polyhaven.com/a/oak_veneer_01): colour (wood-light.webp)
- "White Maple Veneer" by Jenelle van Heerden
  (https://polyhaven.com/a/white_maple_veneer): colour (maple.webp)
- "Black Walnut Veneer 01" by Jenelle van Heerden
  (https://polyhaven.com/a/black_walnut_veneer_01): colour (walnut.webp)
- "Caban" by colormass and Rico Cilliers
  (https://polyhaven.com/a/caban): normal map (cloth-normal.webp)

Changes: reduced to 512 or 768 pixels and stored as WebP; the two woods
graded to the colours the tables were painted in, the oak turned a quarter
round; the veneers are tinted by the game.

Grounds and walls round the tables, also Poly Haven textures under CC0:

- "Dirt Floor" by eye-candy.xyz (https://polyhaven.com/a/dirt_floor):
  colour and normal map (src/assets/tex/earth.webp, earth-normal.webp)
- "Leafy Grass" by Charlotte Baglioni (https://polyhaven.com/a/leafy_grass):
  colour and normal map (grass.webp, grass-normal.webp)
- "Large Red Bricks" by Rob Tuytel
  (https://polyhaven.com/a/large_red_bricks): colour and normal map
  (brick.webp, brick-normal.webp)
- "Concrete Floor 02" by Rob Tuytel
  (https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_floor_02): colour (stone.webp)
- "Gravel Floor 02" by Jenelle van Heerden and Dimitrios Savva
  (https://polyhaven.com/a/gravel_floor_02): colour and normal map
  (gravel.webp, gravel-normal.webp)
- "Laminate Floor 02" by Dario Barresi and Charlotte Baglioni
  (https://polyhaven.com/a/laminate_floor_02): colour (boards.webp)

Changes: reduced and stored as WebP; each colour picture brought to a
middling grey so that the game lays its figure over its own colour.

The lifelike look (src/engine/lifelike.ts), also Poly Haven assets under CC0:

- "Poly Wool Herringbone" by colormass, Rico Cilliers
  (https://polyhaven.com/a/poly_wool_herringbone): colour, normal and
  roughness (src/assets/tex/wool.webp, wool-normal.webp, wool-rough.webp)
- "Rough Linen" by colormass, Rico Cilliers
  (https://polyhaven.com/a/rough_linen): colour, normal and roughness
  (linen.webp, linen-normal.webp, linen-rough.webp)
- "Brown Leather" by Rob Tuytel (https://polyhaven.com/a/brown_leather):
  colour, normal and roughness (leather.webp, leather-normal.webp,
  leather-rough.webp)
- "Metal Plate" by Rob Tuytel (https://polyhaven.com/a/metal_plate):
  colour, normal and roughness (metal.webp, metal-normal.webp,
  metal-rough.webp)
- "Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy (Pure Sky)" by Greg Zaal, Jarod Guest
  (https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky): HDRI
  (src/assets/env/sky.hdr)
- "Cowboy Town Saloon" by Dimitrios Savva, Jarod Guest
  (https://polyhaven.com/a/cowboy_town_saloon): HDRI
  (src/assets/env/saloon.hdr)

Changes: the 1k sets reduced to 512×512 WebP (the OpenGL normal map), the
colour maps made grey, evened and levelled to 0.78 so the game tints them;
the HDRIs reduced from 1k to 512×256 Radiance files with values capped at 48.
