Photographed textures

The table top of the meat grill is the diffuse map of "Wood Table" by
Dimitrios Savva (Poly Haven, https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_table), released
under Creative Commons Zero (CC0),
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Changes: reduced to 768 x 768 and stored as WebP (src/games/grill/table.webp).

The raw flesh of four of its cuts is cut out of three photographs from the
WordPress Photo Directory (https://wordpress.org/photos/), all released
under Creative Commons Zero (CC0):

- pork belly: photograph by Yam B Chhetri,
  https://wordpress.org/photos/photo/5306a119c9/
- marinated rib: photograph by Yam B Chhetri,
  https://wordpress.org/photos/photo/3076a11a25/
- brisket and neck: photograph by Vishnu Chandra,
  https://wordpress.org/photos/photo/9496a09869/

Changes: a square of flesh taken from each, reduced to 320 x 320 and stored
as WebP (src/games/grill/belly.webp, rib.webp, brisket.webp, neck.webp); the
neck made paler; the colour of cooking is laid over them by the game.
