# Development instructions

- Use the existing checkout. Cloud tasks are isolated; do not create a worktree unless explicitly requested.
- Use Node.js 24 LTS and npm. Install with `npm ci` (in this cloud, `--cache /tmp/wtg-npm` if needed).
- Start with `npm run dev`. Production check: `npm run build`. Rules: `npm test`. Browser: `CHROMIUM_PATH=/usr/bin/chromium npx playwright test tests/e2e/<game>.spec.ts` for the specs of what changed (install Playwright Chromium locally). Do not run the whole browser suite unless the user asks for it.
- Keep rules and AI independent of Three.js and DOM. All moves must pass through `play`.
- Only settings, AI preferences and gomoku results are stored (versioned keys). No game is saved or resumed: the user asked for that to be removed, so do not add it back unasked.
- On the four board games a tap places or moves at once, like a click (the user asked for this; do not bring back the confirm step on touch). Preserve camera-transition input blocking and engine resource disposal.
- Keep unimplemented games visibly disabled. No automatic public deployment or repository visibility changes.
- Update README and ROADMAP for material changes. Test rule changes with Vitest and input/UI changes with Playwright.
- Reversi rules live in `src/games/reversi/rules.ts`: use shared legal-move generation for sessions and search, reconstruct automatic passes during replay, and keep the Reversi preference key separate from Gomoku.
- Browser Reversi AI must drain `searchSteps` cooperatively in its Worker. Mark exact endgames solved only after the full remaining placement depth completes; passes do not consume placement depth.
- Preserve nonblocking GPU startup readiness and scene/audio cleanup. Reversi AI evaluation: `npm run reversi:evaluate` (40 paired-color games at the documented budget scale plus timing samples).
- Chess uses pinned chess.js 1.4.0 through `src/games/chess/rules.ts`. Do not use its automatic threefold/50-move `isGameOver` policy; sessions implement claims separately from fivefold/75-move automatic draws.
- Chess sessions and preferences live only in memory: no persistence, resume feature, AI-vs-AI league, or Elo estimate. `npm run chess:measure` measures individual positions only.
- Keep chess repetition context and halfmove clocks in transposition keys, checked quiescence nodes searching evasions, and the chess.js BSD notice in exported artifacts.
- Every game's undo rebuilds the state through `undo` in its `rules.ts` (legal replay; Reversi reconstructs passes, chess replays from `initialFen`); cancel the opponent's Worker before replacing the state. Results against the computer are counted once, in `accept`.
- `scripts/export-html.mjs` starts the embedded Workers as classic Workers so the single HTML works from `file://`; it must fail loudly if a Worker bundle keeps module syntax.
- Camera distance comes only from `cameraDistance` in `src/engine/camera.ts`; views and e2e click helpers must both use it. Stones, discs and marker rings share per-view geometry/materials (`BoardView.assets`): never dispose them when removing a mesh, only in `dispose`.
- Platform chrome (header, collection, setup panels, dialogs) is styled in the "Platform skin" section at the end of `src/style.css`, scoped to `header`, `.library` and `dialog`; keep game-table styles out of it. Mode radios must stay real, full-size inputs under their pills.
- Blackjack rules live in `src/games/blackjack/rules.ts` and `shoe.ts`, free of Three.js and DOM. Each action is one atomic transition (deal through settlement), so nothing can be dealt or settled twice; refused actions return `null` and never mutate their input. No persistence, undo, difficulty or card controls in the UI.
- The blackjack view receives hidden cards without a face (`face: null`); never put the hole card's rank, suit or total into the DOM, an accessible name or a data attribute before it is revealed. Tests fix the card order by stubbing `crypto.getRandomValues` with `tests/blackjack-fixtures.ts`, not through game code.
- Yacht dice rules live in `src/games/yacht/rules.ts`; its computer (`ai.ts`) is an exact expected-value search over the 252 dice hands that runs synchronously, so it needs no Worker. Apply its advice only through `roll`, `toggleHold` and `score`. No persistence or undo. Tests fix the faces by stubbing `crypto.getRandomValues` with `tests/yacht-fixtures.ts`.
- Every catalog game is now playable; a new catalog entry must stay visibly disabled until it is implemented.
- Seotda and seven-card stud share `src/games/cards/` (betting engine with side pots, `BettingGame` adapter, `CardTableView`) and `src/ui/betting-game.ts`. A new betting game supplies rules, a `BettingGame` adapter and a `CardSkin`; do not fork the table screen. Other seats' face-down cards carry no face or text until a showdown they reach.
- The hwatu pictures under `src/games/seotda/cards/` are CC BY-SA 4.0 from Wikimedia Commons; keep `public/hwatu-license.txt` and its copy in the exported HTML. Any new third-party asset must be freely licensed, inlined (no runtime requests) and credited the same way.
- Janggi rules live in `src/games/janggi/rules.ts` (board as a 90-character string; passes are legal moves). Its search runs in a Worker through `JanggiAIClient`; the tree uses pseudo-legal moves with king capture as the win and must keep considering the pass.
- Yacht dice, seotda and seven-card stud play their computer seats at the fixed "normal" level; do not add a difficulty control or a yacht hint back without being asked. The engines keep the five profiles for tests.
- A card seat 0 must uncover by hand is marked `peek` by the game; the table screen withholds its text and the hand name until `peeked`, in the DOM as well as on the felt. Folded hands stay at their seat with `face: null`.
- Local two-player chess and janggi turn the table to the player to move (`setCamera(..., delay)` with `orbit`); e2e click helpers must read `data-facing`. Gomoku and Reversi never turn: do not add it back without being asked. Keyboard arrows are relative to the viewer.
- Choices in betting games carry the card ids they stand for (`Prompt.options[].cards`); the table offers those cards and the deck through `CardTableView.setChoices`, and a tap must go through the same `act` path as the button. Seat 0's `secret` cards are drawn shaded.
- Pointer-to-ray conversion is `aim` in `src/engine/scene-utils.ts`; do not copy it into a view.
- Yut nori rules live in `src/games/yut/rules.ts` (stations 0–28, `WAITING`, `FINISHED`; a move turns onto a short road only when it starts on a corner or the centre). Its computer (`ai.ts`) is a synchronous one-move evaluation. The yard in `view.ts` is fully procedural: add no external asset without a licence notice. Tests fix the throws with `tests/yut-fixtures.ts`.
- `mirrorStatus` (`src/ui/banner.ts`) repeats `#turn` inside `.board-area` as `.board-hint` (aria-hidden; the side panel stays the live region). Anything withheld from `#turn` is therefore withheld there too: never write to `.board-hint` directly.
- The collection is a horizontal rail (`#game-rail`) with the setup panel (`#game-detail` inside `.game-stage`) below it; tiles keep their `… 살펴보기` names and `aria-expanded`. Rail callbacks can fire after the collection is left: guard them with `rail.isConnected`.
- Blackjack and stud share one deck: `src/games/cards/faces.ts` (`CARD`, `drawFace`, `drawBack`) with the pictures under `src/games/cards/faces/`, CC0 (Dmitry Fomin, Wikimedia Commons). A new card game must use it rather than draw its own cards. Keep `public/cards-license.txt` and its copy in the exported HTML. In three-card seotda seat 0 picks its two cards only after the last bet (`conclude`).
- Settings round a table are procedural: `parkBench` and `cabin` in `src/engine/scenery.ts`, the casino table in the blackjack view (one per layout; its felt must contain every card and the stake ring). Scenery must not hide the playing surface from the camera or cast large shadows on it. The yacht tray's size is fixed in its view, not derived from `SUBJECTS.yacht`, which is wider to show the cabin.
- Hexa germ war, nine men's morris and gonu are `PointGame`s (`src/games/points/types.ts`): rules in `src/games/{hexa,morris,gonu}/rules.ts`, a `BoardSpec` in `board.ts`, texts in `config.ts`; they share `points/ai.ts`, `points/view.ts` and `src/ui/point-game.ts`. A new game of this kind supplies those three files; do not fork the screen. Their computer has three levels (`LEVELS` in `points/ai.ts`). Gonu marks only the piece picked up, never every movable piece (`showMovable: false`; the user asked for this). Cham gonu runs on the morris rules with corners joined (`inner`). A move is `{from, to, extra}` chosen in that order, and the view animates only what `effect` reports. `hanokYard` in `scenery.ts` is shared by yut and gonu.
- Checkers, dots and boxes, hex, mancala and snakes and ladders are `PointGame`s too. A game drawn its own way passes `view` (a `PointView`) instead of `board`, with `colors` and `keyPoints`; dots and hex share `PaperView` (`src/games/paper/view.ts`, repainted on a canvas). Games of chance draw from the `random` passed to `play`; `action` adds a button for a single legal move.
- Spider solitaire (`src/games/spider/`, `src/ui/spider.ts`) and the world tour (`src/games/tour/`, `src/ui/tour.ts`) have their own screens. Layout numbers shared with browser tests live in files free of Vite-only imports (`spider/layout.ts`), because `faces.ts` uses `import.meta.glob`.
- The world tour is an original property game: do not copy the board, city list, cards or names of an existing product. Its flags are drawn in `tour/flags.ts`; buildings are pieces set down one beside the other (`raise`). The names 황금열쇠 and 무인도 for the card and waiting spaces were asked for by the user; everything else stays its own. Likewise 우주여행, 콜롬비아호 and 인류평화기금 and their rules. A golden key is drawn (`draw`), turned over on the board (`revealCard`), read, then carried out (`follow`); my own choices (buy, build, travel) are put in dialogs by `ask`, and a computer's card is shown in a dialog that closes by itself. A traveller's deeds open from the side list (`deeds`); `sell` works only in the owner's turn. Its cash, sparkle, wave and space sounds are synthesised in `sound.ts`. Spider cards move by drag and drop only (`onDrop`), never by a bare tap, and show the shared faces untouched (no rank band painted over them: the user asked). The tour board has 40 spaces, ten to a side (`SIDE = 10`); deeds show each place's flag (`drawFlag`).
- Collection thumbnails are captures of each game's own canvas (`src/assets/thumbs/<id>.webp`, via `src/ui/thumbs.ts`); after changing how a table looks, recapture with `scripts/capture-thumbs.mjs` (against `vite preview --port 4175`) and reduce to 960×720 WebP. The script sets `data-capture` on `<html>`; screens that draw selection marks must leave them out while `capturing()` (`src/ui/banner.ts`) is true. A new game needs its own capture.
- Recorded sounds under `src/engine/sounds/` are CC0 (Kenney); keep `public/sound-license.txt` and the synthesised fallback.

- Gomoku, Reversi and chess have no hint and chess and janggi no move list: the user asked for their removal. Janggi and spider keep their hints.

- Gomoku's double three (삼삼) is refused only when `State.noDoubleThree` is set (`doubleThree` in its rules, both colours, five still wins); the search filters such points at the root only.
- World tour: a turn ends only through `endTurn` (phase `end`, the 턴 종료 button on the board; computers call it themselves), so a traveller can sell before passing the dice. A free place is offered even when money is short (`decide(yes)` refuses until it is enough). A stay costs 1.5 × the outlay, units dropped (`feeOf`). Golden keys (`LUCK`) keep their own wording and amounts; tickets and waivers are held on the player.
- Every 게임 방법 window follows one order built from `src/ui/guide.ts` (`part`, `steps`, `table`, `notes`): 기본 규칙, the game's own terms in tables, 처음이라면. - Battleship (`src/games/seabattle/`), four in a row, twist five and hare and hounds are `PointGame`s whose views extend `TableView` (`src/games/table/view.ts`): a view builds its scene and supplies `subject`, `spots`, `place`, `animate` and `mark`; do not copy the renderer loop again. A game with hidden pieces sets `PointGame.choose`, which may use only what the player to move can see (`aimShot` never reads where unsunk ships lie beyond hit or miss); battleship writes nothing about the fleets into the DOM, begins with side 0 laying its ships (phase `place`; `tools` are buttons for moves to points off the board) and is `computerOnly`: do not bring back a table for two. - Hit and blow (`hitblow/`, titled 히트 앤 블로 at the user's request; `repeat` allows a digit more than once), wall race, dominoes, alkkagi, carrom and tile rummy are `PointGame`s on `TableView` too. Tools (`config.tools`) are drawn inside `.board-area` (`.pg-tools`), never in the side panel: the user asked. Alkkagi and carrom share `src/games/flick/` (fixed-step disc physics run by rules and view alike, so a shot is deterministic; `FlickView` turns a pull on a disc into a move through `onMove`); the janggi board's two hinges are fixed posts in its arena. Games with something hidden or a shot that cannot be taken back set `computerOnly` and/or `noUndo`. Wall race and tile rummy keep generic titles and name the product they resemble only in their intro.
- Tile rummy's computer may take the table apart (`rebuild`, cached per state; legal for side 1 only, and not at the easy level): `partition` must use every tile it is given, and sets holding a joker are left as they stand.
- In tile rummy a tap picks a tile up or puts it back (`State.picked`); what is picked is then laid as a new set (`NEW_SET`, rack tiles only), regrouped (`REGROUP`, with tiles of the table) or set onto a set it fits (`SET_BASE + i`, shown as gold places). The buttons come up only when the picked tiles make a good set: the user asked for this way of playing, so do not go back to moving one tile at a time.
- Alkkagi: a go stone struck at strength 16 or more may fly (`flyChance`, decided by the `random` passed to `play`, recorded in `last.fly` so the view replays it); an airborne disc touches nothing (`Disc.air`). Without `random` nothing flies, which is how the computer thinks its shots through. The user asked for high friction (go 12, janggi 15) and for no sunlight on the tile rummy floor.
- Chinese checkers (`starhop/`), Ludo, pocket ball (`pool/`, eight-ball on the `flick/` physics; a pocket may take only some discs through `only`), Indian poker (`indian/`) and table soccer (`foosball/`) are `PointGame`s on `TableView` too. Chinese checkers and Ludo sit on a white table, pocket ball and table soccer in the pub (`pub` in `scenery.ts`): the user asked. In Indian poker a white lay figure across the table holds its card to its forehead and my own card is shown only from the back; `chooseBet` reads only the card of side 0, and my card's number is never written to the DOM before the round is shown.
- Table soccer is foosball (a rod table, as in the photo the user sent), not flicked discs: do not turn it back. It runs in real time: `foosball/sim.ts` steps the table in fixed slices free of Three.js and DOM, the view runs it every frame (paused while a dialog is open or the page is hidden) and reports only goals through `onMove`; `rules.ts` knows nothing but the score. `view` in a `PointConfig` receives the level as its fifth argument for this.
- Indian poker: one game is one pack of twenty played to the last card (ten rounds), whatever the chips; a side with nothing left still antes what it has and unmatched chips go back (the user asked: do not end the game early again). Each round the figure picks its card up from the table and raises it to its forehead (`lift`, a two-bone arm following the hand in `pose`); keep the cards large.
- Foosball rods go exactly as far as the hand does (`slide` is in table units, each rod stops at its own `travel`; only the computer's hand has a speed limit through `pace`): the user asked. Pocket ball shows the cue behind the white ball while a pull is aimed (`FlickView.aimed`). Ludo tokens sharing a square stand side by side, smaller (`stands`). In Chinese checkers the point opposite counts as filled when every hole is taken and at least one marble there is the mover's, and `candidates` must keep sideways moves when few go forward, or the computer never fills its point.
- The Chinese checkers board's face is a flat disc of its own (`CircleGeometry`): a cylinder cap lays its texture a quarter turn round, which put the drawn holes off the marbles. Do not map it onto the cylinder again.
- Phones play in landscape and the page is always light: the user asked for both. Short wide screens use the "Landscape phones" section at the end of `src/style.css` (no header at a table, the board at full height, the panel scrolling beside it, `.board-hint` hidden). `color-scheme: only light` stays in `index.html` and `:root`; add no dark theme. The Playwright `mobile` project is Pixel 7 landscape: keep it so.
